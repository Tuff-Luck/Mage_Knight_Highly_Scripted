    oe                                                         INTRODUCTION
    Welcome to my custom Print and Play expansion, Life and Death, designed for Mage Knight: The Board Game. This expansion introduces two new knights: a Forest Dryad
    representing Life Magic, and a Necromancer representing Death Magic. Each knight comes with their own unique Action Cards, Skills, Command Tokens, Reference Cards, and
    Player  Turn Tokens.

    This expansion is a free Print and Play project. It will always be free, I ask you do not try and offer me any kind compensation for the work done on it. I hope you like the new
    knights. Feel free to comment in the Release Thread, I would love to hear any thoughts you may have. Comments are the Blogger and Hobby Creator’s crack. I toyed with the
    idea of making this is detailed instructional guide, but I felt it overly complicated it. All the rules you need are printed in the Reference Cards provided. If you have any
    questions, please feel free to contact me via BoardGameGeek in the file section for this project, or the release thread in the Variations Forum

                                                                             As a hero of the Dark Crusaders, worships the Blood Goddess, Dark Tesla. He has delved         deep into
                                                                             forbidden arcane arts with his ruinous experiments to find antHife, the key to his eventual nullification and
                                                                             destruction of the Tesla Spirit and the disillusioned Elementalists who follow it. His quest for antilife has led
                                                                             to the glorious art of necromancy: the ability to raise soulless automatons from the recently deceased.
                                                                         _   Devoid of free will, Mevok’s hordes march, propelled by his own hideous resolve, across the land. Ever
                                                                           _ searching for the Forbidden Forest and the newly dead to feed the flesh pits and expand his ever-growing
                                                                             necrotic legion.

                                                                             Mevok is a Necromancer and has a few additional rules to consider.

                                                                             His entire gameplay resolves around the new game mechanisms for the reanimation of defeated enemies
                                                                             and using them like units during combat. He is also themed around Dark Magic and has a number of skills
                                                                             and abilities that utilise Black Mana, even during the day.

                                                                             Mevok is essentially a Combo Deck. The combo, when it goes off is very, very strong, but it is hard to get
                                                                             everything into place for it to trigger at full strength. The design goal was for it to trigger once every 3 or 4
                                                                             games, When the combo is not functioning, he is aimed at being a mid strength hero in relation to the
                                                                             official Knights.

                                                                             The best way to ensure the triggering of the combo is to focus on Crystal Generation as the main priority
                                                                             for this character. Due to his heavy crystal generation he is a good Spell Caster. Artefact hunting will also
                                                                             greatly  aid in both smoothing the combo trigger and during End Game Scoring.

 Raising undead is a very unpopular act, and each time you resurrect any amount of units as undead, during a single combat, you will suffer a total of -3 reputation. This will lead to
 Mevok quickly hitting X Reputation on the Reputation Track. He dose have a number of skills and cards that help him ignore the X space, allowing him to still interact with locals,
 Still, due to his almost quaranteed loss of the Reputation End Game Scoring tally, it is advisable to seek victory in other categories as your end game focus.
