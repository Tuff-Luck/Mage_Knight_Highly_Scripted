[PAGE 001]
Atlantean
Weather
 A Mage Knight Mini Expansion


[PAGE 002]
Concept
The day/night cycle in Mage Knight is a fantastic and thematic facet of the game. I always
loved how the rolling of the sun or the coming of night really made you feel part of a living,
breathing world. I wanted to add weather patterns to this to help accentuate the day/night
cycle.
Cards
There are two sheets of cards:
1 x Day Card Set
1 x Night Card Set
Print two copies of each sheet, use the corresponding Day or Night Card Back. This will give
you 18 cards in both the Day weather deck, and the Night weather deck. Feel free to use
more Day or Night sheets, but each deck must be a minimum of 18 cards.


[PAGE 003]
Rules
A player will be affected by an Atlantean Weather card rules whenever a weather card is
drawn for the tile they are on, and until they leave the tiles affected by that weather card.
Immediate effects of the card upon a player are resolved as soon as the card is drawn to
determine the weather of the tile that player is on. Ongoing effects are active for as long as
that Atlantean Weather card is in play.

In addition, if the player attacks or interacts with tokens on a different tile to the one they
are on, the rules for the tile the player is on are used for both the player and the target of the
action/attack.

Do not draw an Atlantean Weather card for the starting tile.


[PAGE 004]
Variant 1 - Continental Weather
This variant of Atlantean Weather simulates the weather across large tracts of the Atlantean
Empire. It requires less upkeep that other variants. Feel free to choose your own weather
rows.

A - At the start of each round, draw one day/night weather card for each row.


B – Each card affects the tiles in the row it has been drawn for.
C – At the start of a new round, discard all in-play Atlantean Weather cards, and draw new
    cards from the alternate day or night deck.


[PAGE 005]
Variant 2 - Regional Weather
This variant of Atlantean Weather simulates the weather across individual regions of the
Atlantean Empire. Each tile will have individual weather effects rolling across them over the
course of the game. This variant requires more upkeep.

A – At the start of each round, draw an Atlantean Weather card for the tile each player is on,
and each tile connected to theirs. Unless the card is ‘Clear Skies’, or ‘Rising Moon’ then place
the corresponding weather token in that tile.
B – Place each unique Atlantean Weather card on the Day (or Night) Weather Track. The
Weather Tracks are rules reminders for weather tokens on the tiles.
