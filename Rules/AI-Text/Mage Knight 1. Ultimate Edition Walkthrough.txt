I.                    INTRODUCTION
   The purpose of this book is to explain the game to you in the most natural way. The rules are presented in the order in which you will encounter them in the game, and illustrated by examples and pictures.
   While reading this, you will be able to imagine how the game plays, or, if you prefer, you can play along as you read. In addition to this book, there is also a Rulebook. It contains all the rules of the game, in
    systematical order. Contrary to this Walkthrough, there are no images or examples – the Rulebook assumes that you have read the Walkthrough ﬁrst, and understand all the mechanics. It exists as a fast
                                                                                 lookup for some rules, and to explain ambiguities.

          The second row on the Fame track has this symbol. It
          means that you get one Skill token and one Advanced

The ﬁrst player to advance to level 2 reveals two Skill tokens from
the top of their Skill pile. They should check their Skill description
card to see what they do.
Then, they choose one of these Skill tokens and put it in front of
themself, the other one going to the Common skill oﬀer. Starting
with the next turn, they may use it.
Additionally, the player chooses one of the three cards in the
Advanced Action oﬀer. They put it on top of their Deed deck, and
then replenish the oﬀer with a new card. The cards in the oﬀer are
shifted down and the new one is always added to the top.
When another player reaches level 2, they resolve their Level up
in exactly the same way. However, they now have the option to
choose a Skill token from the Common Skill oﬀer (the ﬁrst player to
Level up did not have this option because the Common Skill oﬀer
was empty).
Learning from other players
After a player reveals their two Skill tokens, they may refuse them
both and instead take one Skill token from the Common Skills
oﬀer, i.e. one that another player did not choose and put there
when leveling up. If they do, they put the chosen Skill token in
front of themself and puts both of the tokens they refused into the
Common Skill oﬀer. They may not choose a Skill of their own that
they previously chose not to take.
